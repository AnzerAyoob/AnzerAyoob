I’m a journalist from the Chenab Valley in Jammu & Kashmir, India, and an independent developer learning by shipping real products. My work sits at the intersection of **public-interest journalism, civic participation, and practical technology**.

I use *Journoist* to describe the intersection of journalism and activism: investigating issues, making information accessible, and building tools that help people participate in public life. I’m interested in technology not just as code, but as something people can use to solve real problems.

## What I work on

- **Journalism & investigations** — reporting, verification, public accountability, culture, and the environment.
- **Civic technology** — tools that help people document issues, understand information, and organise around public-interest concerns.
- **Independent app development** — Flutter and Dart, PHP, MariaDB, Firebase, and practical product engineering.
- **Public information resources** — RTI formats, official letter templates, datasets, and guides designed to be useful beyond a single news cycle.
